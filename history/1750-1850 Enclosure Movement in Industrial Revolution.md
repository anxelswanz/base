The Enclosure Movement refers to the historical process in which aristocrats and landowners in Britain used agreements, violence or parliamentary legislation to consolidate and fence off fragmented peasant holdings and common lands into strictly private farms or pastures.

英国等欧洲国家的贵族和地主通过协议、暴力或议会立法等手段，将原本零碎分散的农民份地及公有地进行围封、合并，转化为绝对私有的大规模集中农场或牧场，从而推动资本主义农业发展并导致大量农民失去土地的历史过程。

1700: approx, 60%-70% of the UK labour force employed in agriculture.
1801: Agricultural workforce share fell to ~35%
1851: Dropped to only ~20% (compared to 50%~60% in continental Europe, e.g. France and Germany)
