
# Background

1832年改革法案的背叛： 1832 年英国国会通过了选举法案，给资产阶级和中产阶级分配了投票权，但把占人口绝大多数的工人阶级排除在外。工人感觉自己被“利用完就丢”，政治不满情绪达到顶峰。 

The betrayal of the Reform Act of 1832: When the British Parliament passed the election bill in 1832, it extended the franchise to the bourgeoisie and the middle class, only to leave out the working-class majority. Feeling cast aside after being used, the workers' political frustration reached a boiling point.

## People's Charter 

In 1838, **William Lovett** and other leaders in London Working Men's Association jointly drafted a political program named: **People's Charter,** this document gave rise to the name of the Chartist Movement.

```
1. Universal Male Suffrage above 21
2. Vote by secret ballot 
3. Payment for members of Parliament so the politics isn't the preserve of the rich
```

“…the laws which make food dear, and those which by making money scarce, makes labour cheap, must be abolished… taxation must be made to fall on property, not on industry… the good of the many, as it is the only legitimate end, so must it be the sole study of the Government.”

“那些推高粮食价格的法律，以及那些通过紧缩货币来贬低劳动力价值的法律，必须被废除……税收必须施加于资产（富人），而非实业（劳动与生产）……多数人的福祉，既是政府唯一合法的终极目标，就必须成为政府施政的唯一课题。”


In 1839, the charters presented a petition to the house of commons with almost 1.3 million signatures, that's 10 percent of the entire population of the time.
