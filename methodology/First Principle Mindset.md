
# Rocket Story 

At the beginning of SpaceX, Elon Musk faced a difficult problem: rockets are very expensive. If people use `analogy` mindset, that would be grounding the fundamental truth in `buying the rocket` , that is negotiating price with Russians. 

But what Elon saw is the principles that are inside that mindset `buying the rocket`. Musk removed the constraint that the "product must be purchased as the finished products from external suppliers". He no longer focuses on "negoation" or "down-sizing", he looked at the fundamental realities of "raw materials" or "law of physics".

- Identifying the fundamental truths 
- Reasoning from there and reconstruct
