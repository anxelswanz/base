## Thomas Boulsover 

THomas Boulsover made the chance discovery that the fusion of copper and silver can work as a single metal that has the same external experience as silver.

His method helped reduce the production cost to one third or a quarter of those sterling silver (纯银), enabling middle class people to afford luxurious silverware.

His invention turn the Sheffield from a simple cutlery town to Europe's top manufacturing hub for silverware and metalware.
