
# 1. Definition 

**DDR** 

Deutsche Demokratische Republik (also known as GDR German Democratic Republic)

**BRD** 

Bundesrepublik Deutschland 德意志联邦共和国 (also known as Federal Republic of Germany)

- 1945 
	Nazi was defeated, and the whole Germany was split by allies (U.S, UK, France and Soviet Union)
- 1949
	Germany was officially divided into two nations: East and West.
- 1961 
	Because of a lot of people moved from East Germany to West Germany, the Berlin Wall was established
- 1989 
	The fall of Berlin Wall
- 1990  
	1990.10.3 marked reunion of East and West. This day later became Germay's National Day.
