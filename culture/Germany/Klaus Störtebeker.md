**Klaus Störtebeker** (c. 1360 – 1401) was the most famous and legendary pirate leader of the Hanseatic era in Northern Germany. He was famous for robbing the rich and give to the poor. In 1401, he was captured by a fleet from the Hanseatic city of Hamburg and sentenced to death by decapitation. The macabre legend of his headless body walking past his crew members to save them from execution cemented his status as a permanent cultural icon in Northern Germany.
