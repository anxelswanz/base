
# 1. Definition 

Originally, the word "Hansa" comes from Old High German and Gothic roots, and tis earliest literal meaning was "a band of man" or "a fellowship".

A trans-regional **mercantile and defensive alliance** spontaneously formed by northern German cities and merchant groups from the 12th to the 17th centuries.

# 2. The purpose of building Hanseatic League 

1. Members jointly funding the escorts against piracy.共同出资建立武装护航防范海盗
2. Monopolizing baltic sea trade, externally exclude opponents such as Netherlands and the UK.垄断波罗地海的贸易垄断地位

# 3. Main trade commodities 

Salt (Northern Germany -> Northern Europe)
Herring (Scandinavia -> Inland Europe)
Beer & Grain (Northern Germany -> England and Flanders)
Timber (Russia -> Western Europe)
Woolen cloth (England and Flanders -> Eastern Europe)

# 5. Hansetag

It was supreme ruling assembly and decision-making body of the medieval Hanseatic League, where representatives of member cities gathered (mostly in Lübeck) to debate and vote on collective policies.


In Lübeck, only 2.5% of inhabitants are eligible for election to the council (Patrician). Council members are selected from among this group.
