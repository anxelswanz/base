
- **Total Captured:** During World War II, the invading German forces (both the Wehrmacht and the SS) captured approximately **5.7 million** Soviet Red Army officers and soldiers.
    
- **Death Toll:** Among them, **2.8 to 3.3 million** were brutally murdered under Nazi captivity and abuse, resulting in a staggering mortality rate of **57% to 58%**.
    
- **By Contrast:** The mortality rate of Western Allied POWs (such as British and American soldiers) in German concentration camps was only around **3%**. This stark contrast fully demonstrates the Nazis' highly targeted genocidal intent against the Slavic peoples.
