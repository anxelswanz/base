**民族共同体 People's community

指纳粹德国时期（1933–1945）的核心政治与种族意识形态。该概念宣称要消除阶级、宗教和地域的对立，将所有“纯洁的阿利安人”团结为一个利益高度统一的整体。然而，其本质是一个**极具排他性和压迫性**的极权工具，其建立完全基于对犹太人、罗姆人及政治异见人士等“非我族类”的残酷隔离、剥夺与迫害。

A core ideological concept in Nazi Germany aimed at creating a classless, socially unified society of "racially pure" Aryans. Nazi claimed they will end class conflict and build a unified people's community. The core principle is "All men are not equal", those who are against Nazi are perceived as "enemies of the state". And it unified society of "racially pure" Aryans and exclude other "racial inferiors".
