
SS (党卫军) was initially a small elite group unit in [[SA]]. Under the leadership of Heinrich Himmler, it served as massive the primary instrument of terror and massive murder. The SS controlled the entire police state (Gestapo), operating Concentration Camps (executing holocast) and commanded its own elite military: Waffen-SS
