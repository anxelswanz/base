During the [[1750-1850 Enclosure Movement in Industrial Revolution]] in the UK, many farmers and herders had their land taken by nobility. Seeing that factories in first [[1760-1840 First Industrial revolution]] offered better pay than farming, they migrated to cities to work. However, as productivity rose, the factory owners had to slash the wages and increase the working time to maximize the profits, leaving workers earning just enough to barely survive. Having lost their lands and nowhere to return, the workers plunged into destitute poverty.
