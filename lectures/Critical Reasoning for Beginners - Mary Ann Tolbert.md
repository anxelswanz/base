## An  [[Arguments]]


What makes a sentence a a premise or a conclusion is the function/role that they are playing not anything intrinsic to the sentence. If it's playing the role of being a sentence for which you are arguing, then it's a [[Conclusion]]. If it's playing the role of being a sentence for which you are offering a reason for believing the other one, then that is a [[Premise]].


## An [[Assertion]]

Some assertions look very like arguments.

If it snows tomorrow then the mail will be late. 
**It is a single statement (a conditional assertion):** It claims that a specific relationship holds true—_if_ condition A happens, _then_ outcome B will follow. You are asserting a single fact about how the weather will affect the mail.

## Facts
