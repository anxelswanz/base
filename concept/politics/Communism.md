
In political theory (particularly Marxist thought), socialism is the transition state between capitalism and communism.

| **Feature**               | **Socialism**                                                                                                                                     | **Communism**                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Property & Ownership**  | Major industries and infrastructure are publicly or collectively owned, but personal property (house, clothes, personal savings) remains private. | **All property** is owned collectively by the community. Private property is completely eliminated.               |
| **Resource Distribution** | Resources distributed according to **individual contribution** ("From each according to ability, to each according to contribution").             | Resources distributed purely according to **need** ("From each according to ability, to each according to need"). |
| **State & Government**    | Requires a central state or governmental structure to plan the economy and manage public institutions.                                            | **Stateless and classless.** The state eventually "withers away" as governance becomes self-organized by society. |
| **Method of Change**      | Can be achieved through gradual democratic reform, legislation, and public consensus (or revolution).                                             | Historically relies on rapid, revolutionary overthrow of the capitalist order and state power.                    |
| **Economic Systems**      | Markets and price mechanisms can still play a limited role alongside public planning.                                                             | Money, market exchange, and wage labor are entirely abolished.                                                    |
