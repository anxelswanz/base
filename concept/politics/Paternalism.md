
# Strong Paternalism

refers to a governance or ethical principle where the state or authority intervenes in a competent adult's choices or limits their liberty for their own good, even when those choices are made knowingly, voluntarily, and with full rational understanding of the consequences.

即使一个成年人具备完全的理性认知能力，且是在知情、自愿的情况下做出的选择，如果政府/权威认为该选择对其有害，也有权直接干预或禁止。

# Weak Paternalism

refers to an approach where the state or authority intervenes in an individual's choices only when the person lacks full rationality, sufficient information, or voluntary agency (e.g., due to youth, ignorance, or coercion), aiming solely to prevent harm that the individual would not have chosen had they been fully informed and rational.

**政府只有在个人“缺乏足够理性/知情权”（如未成年、精神失常、受欺骗、信息不对称、一时冲动）时，才有权干预；如果一个人完全知情且理性，国家应尊重其选择。** 它的目的是帮助人们**纠正信息偏差或暂时冲动**，使人们能真正实现他们**自己内心想要的目标**。
