
It refers to a political system where a hereditary monarch serves as the official Head of State, but their political powers are strictly limited by constitutional laws, unwritten conventions and Parliament.

1. The Head of State and Head of Government

**Head of State** embodies the unity, continuity and sovereignty of the state both domestically and internationally. Their primary focus is diplomacy, ceremonial duties and representing the country. 

**Head of Government** is the chief executive officer who actually runs the country's administration. Their focus is political policy making, drafting budgets and managing government departments.

2. Parliament is the supreme legal authority 

- Parliament can make, unmake or amend any law.
- The monarch cannot override, veto, or refuse to pass laws passed by Parliament.
2. Royal Assent & Political Neutrality

For a bill to become law, it must receive Royal Assent. However, by long-standing constitutional convention, the monarch always grants Royal Assent. 
The monarch remains strictly non-partisan. Never voting or publicly expressing political opinions.
