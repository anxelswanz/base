
A paradox is a statement or an argument that seems self-contradictory, absurd, or logically impossible.


### The Barber Paradox (Russell's Paradox)

> In a town, the barber shaves **all men, and only those men, who do not shave themselves**. Does the barber shave himself?

- **The Problem:**
    
    - If he **shaves himself**, he breaks the rule (he only shaves men who _don't_ shave themselves).
        
    - If he **doesn't shave himself**, he must shave himself (since he shaves _all_ men who don't shave themselves).
        
- **Why it matters:** Bertrand Russell used this simplified riddle to prove that early set theory in mathematics was fundamentally broken, forcing mathematicians to rebuild modern set theory from scratch.
