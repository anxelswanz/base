
Presbyterianism is a mianline Protestant denomination rooted in reformed theological tradition of John Calvin. It is defined by adherence to the absolute sovereignty of God in salvation and its unique form of church government, which is administered by representative councils of elders.

**长老宗（Presbyterianism）**是基督教新教的一个重要宗派，属于加尔文改革宗（Reformed）传统；其在神学上坚信上帝的绝对主权与救恩恩典（如完全败坏等预定论教义），在教会治理上实行由信徒选出“长老”组成各级会议的**代议制民主管理**。
