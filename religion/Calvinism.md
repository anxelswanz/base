father of reform Christianity John Calvin.
Key words: Sovereignty of God, Predestination, The "Five Points", iconoclasm.

- Total Depravity: it refers to the fall has corrupted every aspect of human nature (including reason, emotion, and will), rendering humanity spiritually unable to choose God.

- Unconditional Election: God chooses who to be saved and who to be damned. This selection is based entirely on God's sovereignty and grace, having no relation to human actions, merits or vices thereafter. God selects humans based on "God's good pleasure"
- Calling / Vocation:

	`"...every individual’s sphere of life is a post assigned him by the Lord... Therefore, lest all things should be thrown into confusion by our folly and rashness, he has appointed duties for every man in his particular way of life. And that no one may rashly transgress his limits, he has called these various ways of life 'vocations' or 'callings'." `
	`source: 《基督教要理》（Institutes of the Christian Religion)`
