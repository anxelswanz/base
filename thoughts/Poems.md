2026-10-19 Three Cranes Hotel, Sheffield

**Not**  intensity, lest it set my heart pounding,

Let it be stillness, for I have long lean towards peace.

All she carries makes it hazy, and inviting,

while, wistful, I think on what could have been,

For the hours run short, and still I long to hear —

And Eros, it is said, is bittersweet.
