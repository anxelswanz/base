

Displacement 

In industrial revolution, physical labour was mechanized. the physical jobs (hand-loom weaver, croppers and stockingers) are replaced by the machines. A lot of artisans lost the leverage to centralized machine-driven factory. 
In AI era, the cognitive tasks (coding, writing, analysis) are automated, reducing the need for routine knowledge work.

Loss of Ownership

Independent craftspeople were forced into wage labour in centralized factories because they couldn't afford the heavy machinery. Many long-standing family run business shut down as they weren't be able to compete with big factories. 
In AI era, knowledge workers might loss their ownership in domain expertise and specialized intellectual property.

Employment Impact 

Completely destroyed traditional artisan roles, but created new job categories (factory operations, logistics, machine maintenance)
In AI era, it Shifts human labour toward high-level strategy, oversight, domain accountability and interpersonal roles.
