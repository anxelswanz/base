
The VTA is primarily characterized by its large population of dopamine neurons. It **produces dopamine** and ships it out through two major pathways:

|Pathway|Destination|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|**Mesolimbic**|伏隔核 (Nucleus Accumbens / NAc)|Direct "pleasure" and satisfaction. The NAc's core job is translating your **subjective desire into physical action**.|
|**Mesocortical**|前额叶皮层 (Prefrontal Cortex)|Focus, decision-making, planning how to obtain the reward again.|
