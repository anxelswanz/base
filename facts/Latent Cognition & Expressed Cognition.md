# Latent Cognition

refers to unobserved and underlying cognitive schemas and mental models that how people perceive and process information.

For example, when asked about their **stance on** AI development, A and B both expressed objection. However, **A's real concern is that** AI will take over humans' jobs, **while** B thinks **that** creativity and **consciousness** belong **strictly** to humans. Even though they **share the same stance**, they have different latent cognitive structures

## Expressed Cognition

Expressed cognition are arguments, opinions or articulated rationals that human publicly express.
