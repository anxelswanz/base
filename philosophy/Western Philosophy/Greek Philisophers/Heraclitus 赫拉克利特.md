
Heraclitus's most famous view is that all things are in a state of constant change and flux. 

> You could not step twice into the same river.

Yet this change is by no means chaotic, beneath it it lies an objective, universal law and rational principle, that is LOGOS. Heraclitus was the first person that assigns "LOGOS" metaphysial meaning.


upward path and downward path 
upward:  the death of the earth is the birth of water, the death of water is the birth of the air, the death of air is fire. 
downward: For fire by contracting turns into moisture, and this condensing turns into water; water again when congealed turns into earth.
