
Love/Eros is portrayed as supreme power of human experience.

# Sweetbitter Love

>Eros, the limb-loosener, shakes me once again —  
  sweetbitter, unmanageable, a creeping creature.

爱神又一次摇撼我，那松解四肢者，  
甜而苦，无可抵御，悄然爬近的生灵。

Sweet: you are attracted, your fall in love and you  start planning the future of both you staying together, experiencing the wonderful things in life. That desire is so strong and the fantasy is so sweet.

Bitter: if you desire them, they are so distanced and so hard to reach, once you have them, you start worrying that if one day you'll lose them.
