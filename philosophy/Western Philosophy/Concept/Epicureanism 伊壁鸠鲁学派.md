
>Death is nothing to us. When we exist, death is not; and when death exists, we are not.

It's an ancient Greek philosophy founded in Athens around 307 BCE by **Epicurus**. Epicurus taught that pleasure is the ultimate goal of the human life.

# The very definition of pleasure in Epicureanism 

> The absence of pain in the body and of trouble in the soul

- Aponia (无痛):  当身体不饥、不渴、不冷、没有疾病折磨时，肉体就处于一种最完美、最根本的快乐状态. When there's no hunger, thirsty, coldness or illness, the body is in a state of fundamental happiness.

- Ataraxia (灵魂宁静): 当心灵不再害怕死亡、不再害怕神灵的惩罚、不再被虚妄的欲望（如权势、名利）所折磨时，内心所达到的绝对平静。 When the mind is no longer scared of death, the penalty of the god, or the torture of the illusionary desire, it achieves Ataraxia, which is a state of absolute tranquility. ^383d5c
