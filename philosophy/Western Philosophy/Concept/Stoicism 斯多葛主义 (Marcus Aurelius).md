#  The Directing Mind

> The hēgemonikon is the rational, ruling centre of the human soul: the faculty that receives impressions, judges them by giving or withholding assent, and thereby produces our impulses, emotions and actions.

## Receiving and evaluating information
- Filtering impressions received from the external world 
## Passing Judgements
- whether yield to anger or grief, or to maintain composure and objectivity
## Directing action
- guiding one to make rational choices in alignment with virtue, reason and natural order.

```
That is, in stoic opinion, it is not the world of matter or our bodies or the opinion of others that control us, it is reason that by its careful judgement creates an autonomy of the rational self.
```
