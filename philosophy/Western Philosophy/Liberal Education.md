
In late Rome time, Cicero used art liberal.

Trivium, which is the foundation of the humanity, it consists of logic, grammar and rhetoric.
Quadravium, which is the foundation of the science, it consists of arithmatic, geometry, music and astronomy.

The very core purpose of liberal education is not to teach you a specific technical skill but teach you how to become a free person who thinks independently and possesses a well-rounded personality.
