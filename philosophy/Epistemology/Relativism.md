
#subjective

> **Core Definition:** Relativism is the philosophical view that points of view, truth, or morality have **no absolute or universal validity**, but are instead relative to the individual, culture, or historical context.

### Major Historical Figures

- **Protagoras:** The ancient Greek Sophist who declared, _"Man is the measure of all things."_
    
- **Friedrich Nietzsche:** Famously noted that _"there are no facts, only interpretations,"_ laying the groundwork for postmodern relativism.
