
# History in World War 2 

## The Devastating "Operation Gomorrah" (1943)

This operation was jointly executed by the British Royal Air Force and the U.S. Army Air Forces. "Gomorrah", which means the city descroyed by heavenly fire in the Bible brought a catastrophe to Hamburg. It is estimated that during Operation Gomorrah alone, between 34,000 and 43,000 people lost their lives, and over a million left homeless.

### Elb Tunnel 

<u>426 meters long and 24 meters deep</u>

The vast majority of port workers and shipyards (such as the famous Blohm + Voss) were located on the **south bank** of the Elbe (areas like Steinwerder), while the workers' residential quarters were situated on the **north bank** (such as St. Pauli and Altona). Consequently, the city of Hamburg was in desperate need of a cross-river solution that could operate around the clock, regardless of the weather. At the time, constructing a cross-river bridge was considered. However, to allow massive ocean-going cargo ships with heights of dozens of meters to pass through safely, the bridge would have to be built exceptionally high. Given the urban topography and technological constraints of that era, this was highly impractical. Therefore, Hamburg decided to 'go underground' and dig a tunnel beneath the riverbed.

# Transport 


## 1. U-Bahn（Untergrundbahn / 地铁）

- **特点：** 真正的**地下铁**（虽然在郊区也会跑到地面上）。
- **定位：** 主要在**城市内部**运行，站距短、班次极密。


## 2. S-Bahn (StadtSchnellbahn)

- **连通城郊：** 把周围的郊区、卫星城和市中心紧密连接起来。
- **速度快：** 相比于市内的地铁（U-Bahn）和有轨电车（Tram），它的站距更长，运行速度更快。
## 3. RB / RE (Regional-Express 区域快车)

## 4. ICE（Intercity-Express / 城际特快列车）

- **特点：** 德国铁路的**高铁**（大白车，车身有一条红线），是德铁最高级别、速度最快的列车，时速可达 200-300+ 公里。
- **定位：** 连接**德国各大核心城市**（如柏林、法兰克福、慕尼黑）以及邻国（如奥地利、瑞士、法国）。车内设施最舒适，票价也最贵。
