
# Places 

## 1. Brandenburg Gate （1788—1791）

King Frederick William II of Prussia ordered the construction of the Brandenburg Gate as a triumphal arch-style entrance to Unter den Linden.
普鲁士国王腓特烈·威廉二世下令建造勃兰登堡门，以此作为通往菩提树下大街的凯旋门式入口

![[37d83193725050bc8f2271365e26360a.jpg]]

the 'Quadriga'—a four-horse chariot sculpted by Johann Gottfried Schadow—was placed atop the gate. The goddess, holding a sceptre adorned with the Prussian eagle and the Iron Cross, drives the chariot as it slowly enters Berlin.

1793年，门顶安放了由沙多（Johann Gottfried Schadow）雕刻的“胜利女神四马战车”（Quadriga）。女神手持带有普鲁士鹰和铁十字勋章的权杖，驾驭着四马战车缓缓驶入柏林。
