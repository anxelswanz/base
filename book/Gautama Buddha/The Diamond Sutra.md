
> 菩萨于法，应无所住，**行于布施。所谓不住色布施**，不住声香味触法布施。须菩提，菩萨应如是布施，不住于相。
> A Bodhisattva should practice generosity without clinging to anything. That is to say, practice generosity without attachment to forms, and without attachment to sounds, smells, tastes, touch, or thoughts. Subhuti, a Bodhisattva should give in this manner, unattached to appearances.
