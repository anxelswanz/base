

## Pausanias 保塞尼亚斯: 

- Earthly love: the love of body and pleasure
- Heavenly love: It comes from the Urania Aphrodite. This kind of love requires both party to advance toward virtue, knowledge and wisdom

## Aristophanes 

In the ancient times, humans look different from how they do now. a human had four legs, four arms, two heads, two faces and two genitals perched on two perfectly spherical body. There were pure male, pure women and the androgynous. The pure male was formed by two male bodies (after being cleaved, they became the modern men who are exclusively attracted to male). The pure female was formed by two female bodies (after being cleaved, they became the modern women who are exclusively attracted to women). The androgynous was formed by a male and a female and after cleaved they are attracted to the opposite sex.

The spherical humans were super powerful and extremely ambitious, they mounted an assaulted on the Mountains Olympus in an attempt to displace the gods. This attempt failed and Zeus cut the spherical humans in half.  

In Aristophane's opnion, love is the pursuit of the wholeness and the desire to be reunited with one's the other half.


Diotima

- Eros is neither human or god, instead, he's a daemon between these two. Eros is the combination of Poros and Penia. 
- The love in its essence is the middle state.
- Beauty is first and foremost eternal, it neither grows or ages, nor does it rise or fall. -> Diotima's Ladder of Love.
- The right path of pursuing beauty is under the guidance of love, going from earthly beauty of body to beauty of behavior and then the beauty of mind.

Diotima's Ladder of Love 
- Love of a particular physical body
- Love of all physical body 
- Love of souls 
- Love of laws, institutions and activities
- Love of Knowledge, sciences
- Love of the absolute beauty
