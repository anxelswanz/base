
谢菲尔德位于 **5 条主要河流** 的交汇处。

这 5 条河流分别是：

1. **唐河（River Don）：** 主干河流，其余 4 条河流最终都汇入唐河。
    
2. **希夫河（River Sheaf）：** **谢菲尔德（Sheffield）的市名正来源于此**。它在市中心（历史上的谢菲尔德城堡遗址附近）汇入唐河。
    
3. **波特溪（Porter Brook）：** 在谢菲尔德火车站下方汇入希夫河。
    
4. **洛克斯利河（River Loxley）：** 流经西北部，汇入唐河。
    
5. **里夫林河（River Rivelin）：** 快速流动的山涧，先汇入洛克斯利河，再一同注入唐河。

### The sweatshop system 

Because the "little mesters" possessed technical expertise but lacked bargaining power, big manufacturers—knowing these independent craftsmen could not afford to hold out indefinitely—refused to buy goods at traditionally fair and reasonable prices. This forced the little mesters to undercut one another's quotes, triggering a vicious price war.

To describe this situation, the story of Sheffield used a phrase: "cutting one another's throats."

The cutlery industry was increasingly of outworkers earning depressed wages, who locked into relationships of dependency with manufacturers who controlled access to external market.  An even worse practice was the "pawning of the workers". In times of price depression, manufacturers would keep the rents high and workers were forced to agree to work off large debts. (p155)

This was a profound disrespect to the independent cutlers and stood in stark contrast to Sheffield's proud heyday. (P149)

## Bessemer Method 

To put it in chemical terms, pig iron contains around **3.5%–4.5% carbon**, which makes it hard but extremely brittle. The Bessemer converter forces oxygen through the liquid iron, triggering this core reaction:

$$C + \frac{1}{2}O_2 \rightarrow CO \uparrow$$

The resulting carbon monoxide ($CO$) burns off at the mouth of the vessel in a towering flame. By burning off almost all of this excess carbon, the carbon level drops down to between **0.1% and 1.5%**, converting brittle pig iron into tough, workable steel.
