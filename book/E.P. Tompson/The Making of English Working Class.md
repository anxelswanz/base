## The Formation of Class-Consciousness

The outstanding fact of the period berween 1790 and 1830 is the formation of 'the working class'. This is revealed first, in the growth of class-consciousness: the consciousness of an identity of interests as between all these diverse groups of working people and as against the interests of other classes. (P212)

> The working class made itself as much as it was made. 

That means the working class was forged through the interaction between external structural changes (such as the rise of capitalism and the Industrial Revolution) and the internal agency and self-consciousness of working people.


## The argument from the optimists

The working in 1840 was better in most ways than that of the domestic worker of 1790. The industrial revolution was an age, not of catastrophe or acute class-conflict and class oppression, but of improvement.

The hardships of [[1750-1850 Enclosure Movement in Industrial Revolution]] was due to the war, faulty communications, uncertain markets rather due to exploitation or cut-throat competition.
