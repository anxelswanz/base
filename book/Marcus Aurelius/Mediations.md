# Introduction 

## The Stoic Emperor [[Stoicism 斯多葛主义 (Marcus Aurelius)]]

```
The stoics themselves were not united on every matter of doctrine, but they were one in their conception of divine Providence and a divinely established hierarchy: the great ladder that reached from the inaminate to the animate, from the animal to the human and rational, and, finally from the human to the supreme rationality of the divine.
```

They unanimously agreed that everything that happens is part of "divine providence", "the fate", a rational arrangement designed for the highest good of the whole. That is the reason stoics put forward the idea of living in accordance with the nature.

```
The rationality is the connection between what is divine within the individual human being and the divine fire without. (xxvi)
```

The stoicism is pantheistic (泛神论的). They believe that the universe is not blind or chaotic. The universe is a vast, living and rational organism. This rational force that is governing the universe is known as Logos (Reason). 

The human mind , which is called Hegemonikon, is not isolated, but rather a part, a slice or spark of the cosmic Logos.

##  Merismos (Division, Separation) / Stripping Down 

Analyse the world of men and matter into its parts, and then to reach a conception of the whole of which they are parts. This helps us alienate from the things we value most as humans.

> The roast meat is just the dead body of a bird or pig.
> The wine is just fermented juice of grapes 
> The purple robe is simply the hair of a sheep soaked in shell-fish blood 

Put values back in their proper place so that you are no longer controlled by them.

## Judgement  -> [[Mediations#Revere your power of judgements]]

> Remove the judgement, and you have removed the thought that 'I'm hurt'. Remove the thought ''I am hurt', and the hurt itself is removed.

This sentence is a very good example of how stoic philosopher values the internal judgement of the external trouble. The pains or troubles in mind are only the perceptions we have, whether they are suffering or bless really depends on how we see these perceptions and make rational choices. The removal of judgement requires men to be indifferent to the external things that happen to them whether they are good or bad. As Epictetus famously put it: [[Epictetus 爱比克泰德#^776152]]

>"It is my bad luck that this has happened to me." No, you should rather say: 'It is my good luck that, although this has happened to me, I can bear it without pain, neither crushed by the present nor fearful of the future.'

This is exact example of how men can react to the same external changes differently by putting his own judgement of the things. The pain comes from two places 
- The impact directly imposed by the event
- Fear of the future
Marcus said to himself that men should be like rocky headland on which the waves constantly break whatever things happen. It has the same common ground with  the concept from Epicureanism Ataraxia [[Epicureanism 伊壁鸠鲁学派#^383d5c]] , which means men should not be troubled internally even though two schools of thought are fundamentally opposed. 7

## Book 8

> Regret is a censure of yourself for missing something beneficial. The good must be something beneficial, and of concern to the wholly good person. No wholly good person would regret missing a pleasure. Therefore pleasure is neither beneficial nor good. (8.10)

Good: G(x) (G=Good)
Beneficial: U(x) (U=Useful)
If a good person misses x, they will regret: R(X) (R=Regret) 

Good is beneficial = G(x) -> U(x)
If a person misses something beneficial, then he will regret: U(x) -> R(x)
A good person won't regret for missing pleasure. ¬R(Pleasure)
So pleasure is not useful. ¬U(x) 

This is Modus Tollens (否定后件) The argument is valid.

But is this argument sound?

P1: Good is beneficial (Not much controversy)
P2: If a person means something beneficial, then he will regret. (water is beneficial, but men won't regret for not having a cup of water)
P3: No good person would regret for missing pleasure (What is good person)

The argument is not sound.
