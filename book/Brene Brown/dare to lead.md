# ~={gray}Chapter 2 Clear is kind and unclear is unkind=~

One minute taker is appointed in every meeting, and everyone should take notes. 5 minutes before the end of the meeting, minute taker will read the notes and reach agreement with everyone. The minute taker syncs up with everyone by sharing the meeting notes.

This will
- Create shared responsibility and participation for everyone
- Eliminate guesswork and subjective minutes
- Increase transparency

It's about creating space for understanding different perspectives, learning from everyone around the table and identifying areas where we need to get clear on expectations.

**Leaders must either invest a reasonable amount of time attending to fears and feelings, or squander an unreasonable amount of time trying to manage ineffective and unproductive behaviour.** That means if we find ourselves addressing the same problematic behaviour over and over, we may need to dig deeper to the thinking and feeling driving this kind of behaviour.

## Empathy VS Sympathy 

Empathy is you feel with people, sympathy is you feel for people. Empathy fuels connection, sympathy drives disconnection. 
Sympathy is "I get it, I feel with you"
Empathy is "I feel sorry for you"
