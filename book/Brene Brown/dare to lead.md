- Eliminate guesswork and subjective minutes

## Empathy VS Sympathy 

Empathy is you feel with people, sympathy is you feel for people. Empathy fuels connection, sympathy drives disconnection. 
Sympathy is "I get it, I feel with you"
Empathy is "I feel sorry for you"

Empathy lines:
- Me too.
- I see you, you're not alone.
- I've been in a similar place and it's really hard
- I understand what that's like.
