

# Descartes (1596—1650)


## I think, therefore I am 

Descartes wants to find a starting point for knowledge as unshakable as geometric axiom, so he decided to provisionally treat everything that could be doubted as false.
- The sense is deceptive: A distant tower looks round from afar but turns out to be square up close
- The dream argument: when we dream, we think everything is real. So how can we be sure we are not dreaming right now?
Having pushed his doubts to this extreme, he found that there's one thing that could not be doubted: the "I" that is doing the doubting. I can doubt everything, but the very fact that "I am doing the doubting" proves that I am thinking, and thinking means I exist.

## Cartesian Dualism

### Res cogitans 思维实体

- 指**心灵**或灵魂，它的本质属性是**思维**：怀疑、理解、意愿、想象、感觉等一切意识活动。

 ### Res extensa 广延之物
 - 指**物质**，包括人的身体和整个物理世界，它的本质属性是**广延**：长、宽、高。
- 它**不会思考**，完全按机械规律运动，可以用数学和几何来描述。笛卡尔甚至认为动物的身体也只是复杂的机器。
